About Loky 😉

I'm a Sr. Product Designer who started as a UX developer at LTI before discovering design. That engineering foundation is my edge — I prototype fast, think in systems, and speak fluently with engineering teams.

Along the way, I built an indie game studio (1K+ downloads, 4.9 ⭐ on Play Store), earned an M.Des in Interaction Design from IIT Bombay (IDC), published work at international conferences, and joined Infurnia as founding designer — scaling it from zero to 500+ clients and $1M ARR in under 2 years.

Today I lead design for foundational enterprise security and platform products at Instabase, an AI-native company — and I'm now building SuperApp as a 0→10 product, a multiplayer AI collaboration product reshaping how teams work alongside AI.

I design for the next era of work — where humans and AI build together, not apart.
