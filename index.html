About Loky 😉

I'm a Sr. Product Designer who started as a UX developer at LTI. Coming from engineering, I prototype fast and think in systems, and engineers don't have to translate for me.

Along the way I built an indie game studio (1K+ downloads, 4.9 ⭐ on the Play Store), did an M.Des in Interaction Design at IDC, IIT Bombay, and published at international conferences. Then I joined Infurnia as its founding designer and helped take it from zero to 500+ clients and $1M ARR in 1.5 years.

At Instabase, an AI-native company, I lead design for the core enterprise security and platform products. I'm also building SuperApp, a 0→1→N multiplayer product where teams work together with AI.
